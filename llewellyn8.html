Origins

A Deck Woven from Welsh Myth

First published in 2006 by Llewellyn Publications, this deck was commissioned to honor the company's Welsh-born founder, Llewellyn George, and to celebrate the publisher's enduring legacy. Artist Anna-Marie Ferguson spent years researching the Mabinogion and Arthurian romance to give every archetype a home in Welsh legend.

The deck keeps the bones of the Rider–Waite–Smith system intact — so a reader fluent in traditional tarot can pick it up immediately — while dressing every card in ethereal watercolor landscapes: ancient oak forests, standing stones, misted lakes, and wind-worn coastlines. A handful of titles shift to fit the lore: The High Priestess becomes The Priestess, The Devil becomes The Horned One, and The World becomes The Universe.

The Minor Arcana

Four Suits, Sixty-Five Lives

The 56 Minor Arcana follow the traditional suits — Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles — rendered with softer, more psychological imagery than most decks.

Wands

Fire · creativity, ambition, will, and passion

Cups

Water · emotion, intuition, relationships, and love

Swords

Air · intellect, truth, conflict, and clarity

Pentacles

Earth · work, wealth, the body, and the material world

The Court

Sixteen human archetypes — four per suit — carry the emotional and social layer of a reading.

Page

Eager students and messengers, brimming with raw, untried potential.

Knight

Riders in motion — fast-moving events, fierce focus, restless energy.

Queen

Soft, receptive, and deeply intuitive command of their element.

King

Established rulers who wield outward authority and practical power.
